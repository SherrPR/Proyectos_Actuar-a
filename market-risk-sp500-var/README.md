# Market Risk – S&P 500 Portfolio

## Objective
Analyze the market risk associated with a diversified equity portfolio composed of 20 companies listed in the S&P 500 index.

## Methodology
- Construction of a diversified portfolio using selected S&P 500 stocks.
- Estimation of portfolio returns.
- Risk measurement using different Value at Risk (VaR) methodologies.

## Tools
- Python / R

## Results
The analysis allows comparison of risk levels under different VaR approaches and highlights the impact of diversification on market risk.
