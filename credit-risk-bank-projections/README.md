# Credit Risk – Bank Projections

> Academic project developed as part of a team.

## Objective
Estimate credit risk for the Mexican population by modeling the probability of default using statistical methods.

## Data
Economic and financial variables related to credit behavior in Mexico.

## Methodology
- Data preprocessing and exploratory analysis.
- Development of regression models with penalization methods.
- Estimation of probability of default.
- Evaluation of model performance.

## Tools
- R
- Python

## Results
The models allow identification of relevant variables associated with credit risk and provide projections useful for banking analysis.
