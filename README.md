# Actuarial Projects – Sherlyn Pérez

This repository contains academic projects developed during my Bachelor's degree in Actuarial Science at the
Benemérita Universidad Autónoma de Puebla (BUAP).

The projects focus on financial and banking risk analysis, applying statistical methods and data analysis
to real-world problems.

## Projects
- **Credit Risk – Bank Projections**  
  Development of regression models with penalization methods to estimate probability of default.

- **Liquidity Risk – Mexican Banking System**  
  Analysis of liquidity risk based on demand and time deposits, including macroeconomic variables.

- **Weak Internal Demand in Mexico – Econometric Analysis**  
  VAR(1) model using seven macroeconomic variables to analyze and forecast internal demand behavior.

- **Market Risk – S&P 500 Portfolio**  
  Risk analysis of a diversified portfolio of 20 stocks using different Value at Risk (VaR) methodologies.
