# Liquidity Risk – Mexican Banking System

> Academic project developed as part of a team.

## Objective
Analyze liquidity risk in the Mexican banking system through the relationship between demand deposits,
time deposits, and macroeconomic variables.

## Data
Banking and macroeconomic variables related to savings and liquidity behavior in Mexico.

## Methodology
- Analysis of demand and time deposits.
- Incorporation of macroeconomic variables.
- Statistical modeling and projection of liquidity behavior.

## Tools
- R
- Excel

## Results
The analysis provides insights into liquidity risk dynamics within the Mexican banking system and supports
financial risk evaluation.
